Building a Future-Ready Hub for Every Generation in Westborough

The Westborough Community Center Foundation is raising philanthropic support to create a community hub that strengthens health, connection, and quality of life for all residents.

About Us

Vision

A thriving, intergenerational Westborough where every resident has access to a future-ready community hub that strengthens health, connection, and quality of life — while sustainably reducing long-term taxpayer burden through prevention, collaboration, and shared investment.

Mission

The Westborough Community Center Foundation raises philanthropic support to build and sustain a future-ready, intergenerational community hub that improves health, connection, and quality of life while reducing long-term taxpayer burden.

Our Goals

The Foundation is focused on four interconnected priorities that will create lasting value for the Westborough community.

01

Fund the Center — Raise philanthropic support to build and sustain a community hub.

02

Improve Health — Advance prevention, wellness, and healthy aging.

03

Connect Services — Align partners to deliver integrated programs for all generations.

04

Deliver Impact — Create lasting community value, resilience, and public cost savings.

Our 501(c)(3) Status

The Westborough Community Center Foundation is a federally recognized 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. All donations are tax-deductible to the fullest extent permitted by law under IRS regulations.

501(c)(3) Nonprofit Organization

When you give to WCCF, you are making a tax-deductible investment in the future of Westborough. Please consult your tax advisor regarding the deductibility of your contribution. A receipt will be provided for all donations.
